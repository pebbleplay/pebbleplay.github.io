4. Pity mechanisms (Special Pull only)

  • Guaranteed Legendary: if no Legendary appears in 99 Special Pulls, the 100th pull is a Legendary. The player is then chosen as described above — 50% Featured player, 50% the others (the Featured player is not guaranteed). When a Legendary appears, the counter restarts from 0. The counter is saved and carries over.
  • Legendary Shards: you receive 1 Legendary Shard for every 5 Special Pulls (pulls with Gems, tickets and ads all count equally). With 10 Shards you can exchange for the Legendary player of your choice. This is a guaranteed exchange, not a probability.
  • Duplicates: if you pull a player you already own, the duplicate level rises by 1 (up to level 5), granting +2 skill and +1 growth cap to that player. After level 5, Epic-or-lower players convert into in-game money (Common 50,000 · Rare 200,000 · Epic 800,000; higher with a better store location), and Legendary players convert into 5 Legendary Shards. Theme furniture you already own converts into the same amount of in-game money.
  • Theme furniture sets: placing 3 different pieces of one set on the same floor multiplies that set's visitor types by 1.15 (Cyber Neon: gamers and aspiring pros; Spaceship: students and university students; Hanok: office workers and neighborhood uncles); placing all 5 gives +3% customers overall (total cap +6%). Every piece gives satisfaction +1 (Epic +2; total cap +8). All 15 pieces can also be obtained without pulling: in the Theme Furniture list you can exchange for any piece you do not own with Theme Shards (you get 1 per duplicate pull; Common 10 · Rare 28 · Epic 95) or with in-game money (Common 5,000,000 · Rare 20,000,000 · Epic 80,000,000; +50% per store location tier). Gems are not needed. These exchanges are guaranteed, not probabilities.
  • The Standard Pull has no pity mechanism. A 10-pull has the same odds as ten single pulls, and there is no mechanism that raises the rarity of the 10th pull.
  • Each Standard Pull also gives 1 part shard and each Special Pull gives 3. With 300 part shards you can choose 1 Legendary part of the type you want.
  • New games start with 1 Special Pull Ticket, and 1 ticket is also granted when an old save that had no pull holdings is converted.

5. Pull Tickets and ad pulls

  • Standard Pull Tickets earned in the game (attendance, missions, deliveries, rewards, etc.) have exactly the same odds as the Standard Pull, and Special Pull Tickets have exactly the same odds as the Special Pull. These are also the same as pulling with Gems.
  • Standard Pull via rewarded ad: once per day, with the same odds as one Standard Pull.
  • Special Pull via rewarded ad: once per day, available when 10 or fewer pulls remain until the guaranteed Legendary; same odds as one Special Pull (it also counts toward the pity counter).

10. PC Parts Boxes

In the 'Parts' slot of the Shop's Pull tab you can draw random parts for 50 Gems per draw or 450 Gems per 10 draws. 'Pull 1 more' and 'Pull 10 more' on the result screen cost the same. By watching an ad you can draw once for free per day, and when 20 or fewer draws remain until the 200-draw adjustment you can draw once more per day with an ad. These two ad counts are counted separately from the ad counts for player pulls. Standard and Special Pull Tickets cannot be used on Parts Boxes. Part shard exchange gives the part you choose with certainty, and the first-purchase part selection ticket also gives 1 Epic part of your choice with certainty. Shard exchange does not count toward the box draw count.
