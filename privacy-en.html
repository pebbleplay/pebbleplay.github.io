Privacy Policy

Applies to: PC Café Tycoon (Android) · Effective date: November 18, 2026 (English edition of the Korean privacy policy; if there is any inconsistency in meaning, the Korean version applies to users in Korea and this English version describes our practices for users elsewhere)

Pebble Play (“we”, “us”, the “Company”) is a sole proprietorship (개인사업자) established in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what personal information we process when you play PC Café Tycoon, why we process it, who else processes it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. We also comply with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea (PIPA). The Korean-language policy is available here.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over (see section 9).

1. Who is responsible (controller)

Pebble Play, Republic of Korea. Contact for all privacy matters: pebbleplay.games@gmail.com.

For the purposes of the EU/UK GDPR, Pebble Play is the data controller. Google (Firebase, Google Play, AdMob) acts as our processor for the server and billing services, and as an independent controller for advertising (see section 3).

4. Advertising choices

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). You can manage Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com. Where the law requires consent for personalised ads (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent and you can change your choice at any time in the game settings.

5. International transfers

Our servers are located in Seoul, Republic of Korea (Google Cloud region asia-northeast3), and we operate from Korea. If you play outside Korea, your information is transferred to and processed in Korea. Google may also process data in other countries, including the United States, in providing Firebase, Google Play and AdMob.

  • EEA: The European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision for the Republic of Korea (December 2021) covering commercial operators subject to PIPA. For transfers by Google to countries without an adequacy decision, Google relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and other safeguards described in its terms.
  • UK and Switzerland: we rely on the applicable adequacy regulations or on standard contractual clauses / the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the Swiss addendum, as applicable.

You can contact us (section 12) for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent at any time, and not be subject to certain automated decisions. See the Regional notices below for details by country.

  • Delete your data yourself: Settings › “Delete my data” › confirm twice.
  • Other requests (access, copy, correction, objection, withdrawal of consent): email pebbleplay.games@gmail.com from the address you wish us to respond to and tell us the game name and your nickname (and your user ID if you can find it in the game settings) so we can find your record. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within the period required by the applicable law, and in any case within 30 days where possible (45 days for California requests, extendable as the law permits).
  • Complaints: you may contact your local data protection authority (see Regional notices) or, for users in Korea, the organisations listed in our Korean policy.

Because we do not collect your name or email address in the game, we can only find your records through your user ID or nickname. This may limit what we are able to verify and provide.

8. Deleting your data and account

App name: PC Café Tycoon · Developer: Pebble Play

Option 1. In the app. Settings › “Delete my data” › confirm twice. This deletes your profile, rankings, arena data, in-game mail and guild data stored on our server, and resets the game on this device.

Option 2. By email. If you have uninstalled the app or changed devices, email pebbleplay.games@gmail.com with the game name and your nickname. We will delete your data without undue delay and let you know.

What is deleted: anonymous user ID, nickname, score, ranking and arena team records, mail records, guild or alliance data, and the linked Google account identifier held by us. What may be kept: purchase and payment records, for as long as the law requires (see section 6).

9. Children

PC Café Tycoon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older (and, where a higher age of digital consent applies in your country — up to 16 in some EU member states — for players who have reached that age, or whose parent or guardian has consented). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at pebbleplay.games@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use Google Cloud and Firebase security features, server-side verification of purchases, and rate limiting, and we limit access to the data to what is needed to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date, and where the change is significant or consent is required by law, we will inform you in the game or ask for your consent again.
