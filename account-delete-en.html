Delete your account and data

Apps: PC Café: Seats to Stars and Enhance Tower (Android) · Developer: Pebble Play · Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous ID and keeps your linked Google account ID and email if you link it.

Option 1. In the app (fastest)

Open the game › Settings › Delete my data › confirm. Deletion is scheduled for 15 days later. In PC Café, your rankings, duel listings and café previews are hidden, and you leave your alliance. Sign back into the same account to cancel deletion.

Option 2. On this page (without the app)

If you have uninstalled the game or changed phones, you can still request deletion here with the deletion code that the game shows in Settings › Delete my data › Show deletion code (format PP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). The code is specific to one game and one account. Getting a new code makes the old one invalid. The code is valid for 24 hours and works once.

Option 3. By email (no code)

If you do not have a code, email pebbleplay.games@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, the game name, your nickname, and (if you can) the user ID shown in the game’s settings. We may ask for details only you would know so that we do not delete someone else’s account. We reply within 30 days.

What happens

DeletedNickname, scores and rankings, arena/duel team records, in-game mail, guild or alliance data, shop data, your anonymous user ID (the Firebase Authentication account, together with any linked Google account identifier), and your deletion code. PC Café removes data in its daily cleanup after 15 days (see Option 1). If you play both games with the same account, the sign-in account itself is removed when the last game’s data is deleted.
KeptPurchase and payment records (hashed purchase token, product, status), for as long as refund, fraud-prevention and legal retention duties require (for example, up to 5 years under Korean e-commerce law). They are not linked to a name or email. Short-lived rate-limit counters and standard Google Cloud server logs, which follow Google’s retention settings.
Not affectedData held by Google (Google Play purchase history, AdMob advertising data). Manage it in your Google account.
